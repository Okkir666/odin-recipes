CHICKEN ROLLATINI

Home

Chicken Rollatini is an Italian-American dish made by rolling thin slices of chicken around a filling, then baking them with sauce.

“Rollatini” is an Italian-American culinary term for thinly sliced meat or vegetables that are rolled around a filling. In Italy, similar preparations are more commonly associated with terms like involtini.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb chicken breast, sliced thin
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1/2 cup mozzarella cheese, shredded

INSTRUCTIONS STEP BY STEP

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix the ricotta, Parmesan, egg, garlic, basil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread the mixture on each slice of chicken and roll up tightly.
  4. Place the rolled chicken in a baking dish and pour the marinara sauce over them.
  5. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through and the cheese is bubbly.
